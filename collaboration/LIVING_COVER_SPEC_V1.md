# Living Cover Specification v1

**Status:** approved product direction (owner and ChatGPT, 2026-11-24). It describes where Personal Playground is going. It does not by itself authorize any implementation: each change still needs its own scoped authorization. Known conflicts with what exists today are listed in [HANDOFF](HANDOFF.md) (2026-11-24, creative direction consolidation) and are not resolved here. Companion to [Editorial Strategy v1](EDITORIAL_STRATEGY_V1.md).

Guiding principles:

- **One Playground. Many personalities.**
- **The gallery is the experience. The magazine is the presentation. Personalization makes it yours.**
- **Curated, but not controlled.**

## Purpose

A magazine-style front door to an exploratory gallery and an organized library. It is populated and useful immediately, with no registration, onboarding or personalization required.

## Editorial hierarchy

- The magazine leads.
- A shared editorial cover story establishes a consistent publication identity.
- Personalization shapes the supporting discoveries, not the lead.

## Composition

- Editorial balance: striking imagery and typography, with intentional hierarchy and spacing.
- A visible invitation to explore more discoveries.
- Adapts appropriately to desktop and mobile.

## Supporting discoveries

- Balance familiar interests, adjacent subjects, unexpected content, archival material and current culture.
- Publishing and homepage featuring are separate editorial decisions.

## Refresh rhythm

Layered refresh:

- Keep the lead stable for about five to seven days, or until a meaningful replacement is available.
- Refresh supporting discoveries selectively.
- No forced expiration, arbitrary reshuffling or artificial urgency.

## Atmospheres

- Preserve the original appearance alongside the three atmospheres:
  - Studio: colorful, playful, graphic;
  - The Quiet Room: warm, literary, spacious;
  - The Underground: bold, alternative, poster-inspired.
- Atmospheres may change typography, color, spacing, density, hierarchy and composition.
- They may not change editorial selection, navigation or functionality.
- Use shared components and tokens.

## Discovery pages

Each discovery page has:

- a compelling image and title;
- a concise editorial introduction;
- creator and source attribution;
- a prominent Visit Original action;
- a Save action;
- optional meaningful connections.

The flow is context → choice → exploration.

## Returning visitors

- Subtle continuity: the current cover remains central, with quiet access to saved discoveries.
- Continuity of recent threads requires a separate privacy and value decision.
- No prominent activity dashboard, streaks, visit counters or pressure to finish content.

## Collection

Predictable search, categories and sorting.

## Editions

Historical covers are preserved through references to existing discovery records.

## Saved Discoveries

A straightforward personal shelf, without unnecessary organizational complexity.

## Engineering principles

- Preserve:
  - accessibility;
  - responsive behavior;
  - performance;
  - navigation;
  - owner tools;
  - Sync;
  - existing data boundaries.
- Appearance preferences remain separate from owner Sync.
- Preserve the original appearance unless an alternative is explicitly selected.
