# Personal Playground — vision

No goals. No quotas. Just curiosity.

Personal Playground enriches free time through three complementary pillars: **Go Somewhere** (local discovery), **Do Something** (hobbies and experiences), and **Discover Something** (culture and editorial). Local discovery is a primary practical use case: uncover worthwhile unfamiliar experiences for solo outings, dates and friends in Tampa Bay.

Favor relaxed, flexible outings, independent culture, playful exploration and useful uncertainty labels. Never invent listings or turn leisure into a productivity system. No streaks, engagement optimization, or pressure to consume. Success is finding something worth doing that otherwise would have been missed.

Preserve the existing editorial visual identity, mobile experience, offline support and optional private personalization. Keep hosting and maintenance simple. See [editorial guide](../EDITORIAL.md) for publishing standards.
