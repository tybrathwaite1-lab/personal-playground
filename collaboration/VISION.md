# Personal Playground — vision

## North Star

**Old internet spirit, modern possibilities.**

**Build somewhere worth visiting, not something that demands attention.**

**Reveal the connections. Preserve the wonder.**

The creative baseline is [Creative Blueprint V2](CREATIVE_BLUEPRINT_V2.md) (2026-11-24). It describes four qualities of one experience (Magazine, Curiosity Shop, Gallery, Hideaway) and three experience pillars: the Living Cover (anticipation), the Living Constellation (association) and My Finds (attachment), with the Collection as the published library. It is direction, not implementation authorisation.

## Founding vision (2026-11-17, still in force where compatible)

No goals. No quotas. Just curiosity.

Personal Playground enriches free time through three complementary pillars: **Go Somewhere** (local discovery), **Do Something** (hobbies and experiences), and **Discover Something** (culture and editorial). Local discovery is a primary practical use case: uncover worthwhile unfamiliar experiences for solo outings, dates and friends in Tampa Bay.

Favor relaxed, flexible outings, independent culture, playful exploration and useful uncertainty labels. Never invent listings or turn leisure into a productivity system. No streaks, engagement optimization, or pressure to consume. Success is finding something worth doing that otherwise would have been missed.

Preserve the existing editorial visual identity, mobile experience, offline support and optional private personalization. Keep hosting and maintenance simple. See [editorial guide](../EDITORIAL.md) for publishing standards.

Approved creative direction (2026-11-24): [Creative Blueprint V2](CREATIVE_BLUEPRINT_V2.md), the baseline; [Editorial Strategy v1](EDITORIAL_STRATEGY_V1.md) and [Living Cover Specification v1](LIVING_COVER_SPEC_V1.md), where compatible. How the blueprint relates to the founding pillars above, local discovery included, is an open question (DECISIONS, 2026-11-24).
