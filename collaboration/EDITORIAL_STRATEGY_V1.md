# Editorial Strategy v1

**Status:** approved product direction (owner and ChatGPT, 2026-11-24). It describes where Personal Playground is going. It does not by itself authorize any implementation: each change still needs its own scoped authorization. Known conflicts with what exists today are listed in [HANDOFF](HANDOFF.md) (2026-11-24, creative direction consolidation) and are not resolved here.

Guiding principle: **The library has shelves. Curiosity doesn't.**

## Identity

Personal Playground is an independent culture magazine, an exploratory gallery and a personal library. It is curious by nature, confident in taste and open to surprise. It optimizes for meaningful discovery, not engagement.

## Open discovery

- Categories organize the Playground. They do not define its boundaries.
- The scout must discover, evaluate, recommend and prepare content outside existing categories, without ranking penalties.
- Permanent category additions require owner approval. Flexible topic labels are permitted.

## Quality

- Prioritize discoveries that are fascinating, beautiful, unusual, useful or distinctive.
- Accuracy, source verification, attribution, content rights and functioning links are mandatory publishing gates.
- Cultural significance and connection potential are bonuses, not requirements.

## Sources

- Balance established institutions, publications and archives with independent creators, niche communities and specialist sources.
- Prioritize original creators over popularity.

## Editorial balance

- Maintain a healthy mix of archival and current discoveries. Roughly 60/40 archival to current is guidance, not a quota.
- Distinguish discovery date from creation date.
- Time-sensitive content requires freshness and expiration checks.

## Connections

- Encourage familiar → adjacent → unexpected exploration.
- Where appropriate, offer one to three meaningful, optional connections, with brief context for surprising relationships.
- Reference existing discovery records rather than duplicating content.

Example editorial trail, *Where Underground Culture Finds a Home*: Japanese arcades → underground hip-hop venues → graffiti culture → streetwear.

## Scouting

One shared pipeline handles both category coverage and bounded open-ended exploration. It supports:

- discovery;
- deduplication;
- verification;
- evaluation;
- editorial drafting;
- attribution;
- image preparation;
- suggested connections.

## Automation

- Begin with automated scouting and human batch approval.
- Eventually progress to selective autopublishing, then autonomous editorial operations with exception review.
- Use one shared pipeline with:
  - configurable approval policies;
  - auditability;
  - rollback;
  - pause controls;
  - enforceable quality gates.
- Never lower standards to meet publishing quotas.

## Voice

- Confident, curious, expressive, knowledgeable without pretension, and occasionally playful.
- Provide concise original context and direct visitors toward original work.

## Personalization

- Thoughtful hospitality, not engagement optimization.
- Interests inform discovery without creating filter bubbles.
- Unexpected content remains welcome and invitational.
